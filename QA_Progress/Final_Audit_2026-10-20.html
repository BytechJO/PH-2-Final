411 pages loaded; 319 activity pages covered; 92 content pages. Thirty intro popups opened and closed in the prior round. This round adds 109 activity pages to prior coverage.

Repairs verified

  • Unit 10 adjective activity reserves space for navigation arrows so they do not cover answer choices at 1280px.

Audio outcome

Eight existing clips were linked correctly in the prior round and their targets verified. The user confirmed the content of “Use the words below” and “The cows on the farm”.

  • 10 named sentence references still point to absent files: six in Unit 7 (SB p58), four in Unit 9 (SB p69).

  • 60 popup controls have unset or copied legacy references; correct dialogue/instruction mapping remains a content task.
  • 1,360 placeholder/template references are excluded from recording requests.

All 411 registered pages loaded; 319 activity pages have recorded interaction/open-response coverage and 92 are content pages. Checks cover configured correct answers, reset, and selected negative cases, not every possible wrong answer, every mobile size, or semantic listening to every clip. Free drawing/writing remains teacher-assessed. CVC past-tense activity grades writing only; letter colouring is ungraded. Two requested recordings confirmed by user.
